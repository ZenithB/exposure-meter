Exposure Meter is a photographic light meter and exposure calculator. Measure the light on your scene and read it off as the exposure triangle - aperture, shutter speed and ISO - on three camera-style scrolling wheels.

Two metering modes:

* Incident, using the phone's ambient light sensor.
* Reflected spot metering, using the camera: tap a point to meter it.

There is also a manual EV mode for sunny-16 work or as a fallback.

Lock any of the three parameters and adjusting an unlocked one moves the remaining free parameter, so the exposure stays correct for the metered light. An over/under-exposure badge shows, in thirds of a stop, how far any combination deviates from correct exposure.

Features:

* Exposure compensation, plus or minus 5 EV in thirds.
* ND filter compensation, stackable with exposure compensation.
* Separate calibration offsets for incident and reflected metering.
* Hold to freeze a reading while you set your camera.
* Follows your system light/dark theme, with Material You dynamic colour on Android 12 and above.

Privacy and freedom:

* No internet permission - the app never touches the network.
* No Google Play Services, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
* The only permission is camera, requested only when you first use reflected metering.
